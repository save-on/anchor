->Aspects of video streaming<-
*Bitrate*
- Amount of data used to represent a piece of audio/video
- The higher the bit rate, the more information is transferred and the 
  higher the quality of the audio or video
- Higher bitrate require more storage and bandwidth to transfer

*Adaptive Bitrate Streaming(ABS)*
- Adjust the quality of the video based on the users internet
  speed, ensuring the video can stream smoothly without
  buffering or interruption
- ABS encodes the video at multiple bitrates

*Dynamic-Adaptive-Streaming-over-HTTP(DASH)*
- breaks content into a sequence of small segments, which are served over
  HTTP
- Each segment contains a small interval of playback time
- The content is made into a variety of different bitrates
